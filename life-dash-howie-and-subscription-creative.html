Howie scripts

Five rewrites of the original Howie read. Each one takes a different audience from Creative Directions v2, so none of them repeats the “doctor’s office” angle of the original.

Direction 01

The Medication Blind Spot

Anyone on a daily prescription.

Howie Mandel here. You take the same pill every morning. But is it actually working? LifeDash puts your medications and lab results side by side, so you can finally see the connection.

Direction 02

Beyond the Scale

GLP-1 users.

Howie Mandel here. On a GLP-1? The scale is only one number. Your cholesterol, blood sugar and liver numbers change too. LifeDash tracks your doses and bloodwork together, so you see the whole story.

Direction 06

The Dismissed Woman

Women in perimenopause.

Howie Mandel here. Told “you’re fine” when you know you’re not? Perimenopause touches everything, and every doctor sees one piece. LifeDash brings it all into one place, so you walk in with the full picture.

Direction 08

Day One After Diagnosis

The newly diagnosed.

Howie Mandel here. A new diagnosis means new doctors, new portals and new results. LifeDash gathers it all in one place and organizes it for you, so you know what’s changing and what to ask.

Direction 10

The Multi-Med Juggle

People on several medications, and those caring for a parent.

Howie Mandel here. Three doctors. Five prescriptions. Four portals. None of them talk to each other. LifeDash puts every medication and test result in one dashboard, so nothing slips through the cracks.

Howie ADHD scripts

Five scripts built on Howie’s own public account of living with ADHD. They only use what he has said himself, and none of them claims he takes a particular ADHD medication.

Source: Howie Mandel’s first-person piece for ADDitude, “Howie Mandel Gets Real About his ADHD & OCD”.

Subscription creative

Five directions with no Howie, one for each of America’s five most commonly treated conditions. The messaging moves from “free” to subscription: Life Dash tracks your health all year, not just at a once-a-year checkup.

Ranking and adult figures: AHRQ Medical Expenditure Panel Survey, Statistical Brief #487 (US adults, 2013).

High Blood Pressure

61.1MUS adults treated

Straplines

  • Your blood pressure changes every day. Your checkup sees it once a year.
  • One reading is a snapshot. A subscription is the whole movie.
  • On blood pressure meds? See how your numbers move, month after month.
  • Your heart works 24/7. Your health tracking should too.
  • Stop guessing between appointments. Start tracking all year.

Meta feed ad

You take your blood pressure medication every day. But when did you last see your numbers side by side over a whole year? Most people see one reading at one appointment and hear “keep going.” LifeDash keeps your medications, test results and appointment history organized in one place, every month, so your next visit starts with the full trend instead of a guess.

High Cholesterol

Straplines

  • You take the statin every day. Do you know if your numbers are moving?
  • Cholesterol is a trend, not a single result.
  • Every blood test, every dose, every year. In one place.
  • Your LDL has a story. Your subscription keeps writing it.
  • Annual checkup? Meet your year-round health record.

Meta feed ad

Your cholesterol gets checked, your doctor gives you a number, and the cycle repeats. Nobody shows you the line connecting those numbers. A LifeDash subscription keeps every lab result and medication change organized together, test after test, so you can see whether things are actually moving and ask better questions when you sit down with your doctor.

Mental Health

39.2MUS adults treated

Straplines

  • Finding the right medication takes time. Keep track of every step.
  • Every dose change. Every refill. Every result. One place.
  • Your mental health has a history. Keep it in one place.
  • Not every month is the same. Your record shows the whole year.
  • Bring your whole story to every appointment.

Meta feed ad

Mental health treatment rarely goes in a straight line. Doses get adjusted. Medications change. Months go by between appointments, and it’s hard to remember what happened when. A LifeDash subscription keeps your medications, test results and appointment history organized in one place, all year, so you and your care team can look at what actually changed instead of relying on memory. It doesn’t replace your clinician. It makes the conversation clearer.

Arthritis and Joint Conditions

38.1MUS adults treated

Straplines

  • Your joints have a long memory. Your health record should too.
  • X-rays, scans, prescriptions, specialists. Finally in one place.
  • From your PCP to your orthopedist, everyone sees the same story.
  • Joint care is a marathon. Track every mile.

Meta feed ad

Managing arthritis means a lot of moving parts: your primary care doctor, specialists, physical therapy, imaging reports and prescriptions that change over the years. Each lives somewhere different. A LifeDash subscription keeps all of it uploaded, organized and up to date in one place, so every appointment builds on the last one instead of starting from scratch.

Straplines

  • Your breathing changes with the seasons. Your record keeps up.
  • Inhalers, test results, specialist visits. One place, all year.
  • A good month and a bad month look different. See both.

Living with asthma or COPD means prescriptions, refills, breathing test results and specialist visits that build up year after year. Most of it ends up scattered across portals and paperwork. A LifeDash subscription keeps your full history organized in one place, so you can see how things change over time and walk into every appointment ready to talk about what matters.
